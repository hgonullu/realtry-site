Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · App version: 1.0.0

In short

This app collects nothing about you. There is no account to create. Your choices (language, currency, base month) are stored only on your own device and sent to no server. We, as the developer, cannot see your data.

What the internet connection is used for

The app goes online for one thing: to keep a small public file of published inflation figures and exchange rates (https://realtry.app/data.json) up to date. The file is updated monthly. A copy ships inside the app, and when you are offline the app uses that copy or the most recently downloaded one.

The request carries nothing about you: not your choices, not a device identifier, not an account. As with any request over the internet, the server on the other end (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address; that is outside the app’s control and subject to that organisation’s own policies.

Data sources

  • TurkStat (TÜİK) consumer price index — via the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye EVDS.
  • ENAG (Inflation Research Group) monthly inflation releases — compiled from news reports of ENAG’s announcements; the source of each month is published in sources.json.
  • US consumer price index — FRED / US Bureau of Labor Statistics.
  • Euro-area HICP and monthly average exchange rates — Eurostat.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party tracking software of any kind.
  • No access to location, photos, contacts, calendar or any other permission.

Stored on your device

Your choices (language, currency, base month) are kept in the device’s own storage so the app remembers them next time, and so is a copy of the most recently downloaded data file so the app can work offline. Removing the app removes all of it.

Estimates and not advice

Real TRY is an informational, estimating tool. The “adjusted rate” it shows is a simple calculation that assumes the exchange rate was in balance in the base month you pick; it does not say what the rate should actually be. It is not investment, financial or legal advice. TÜİK and ENAG are the names of their respective organisations; this app is not affiliated with them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text is published on the app’s store page and at this address, and the “last updated” date above changes.
