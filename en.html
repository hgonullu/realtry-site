What should the dollar and euro cost in lira today?

If the Turkish lira had lost as much against the dollar and euro as it lost against goods and services, what would 1 USD and 1 EUR cost in lira today? Real TRY calculates it with two inflation measures side by side: TÜİK (TurkStat, the official CPI) and ENAG (an independent measure).

It does not decide which measure is right; it shows the gap and what it does to the result. The method is relative purchasing power parity, after the Swedish economist Gustav Cassel. An estimate, not investment advice.

The calculation, data sources and limitations are written out on the app’s “Method & Notes” screen. No account, no ads, nothing collected.
